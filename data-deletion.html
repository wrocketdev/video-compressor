Data Deletion

Compress Video for Android · Kwizu Studio · Updated: 8 September 2026

Your videos are processed on your device. We have no server-side copy of them. This page concerns technical analytics, advertising and purchase-related records described in our privacy policy.

1. Local data and future collection

Uninstall the app to stop future activity from this installation. Clear its data from Android Settings → Apps → Compress Video → Storage to reset local preferences, cache and history. Opening the app again can create new records. Saved gallery videos remain under your control. Neither action cancels your Pro purchase or automatically deletes provider-side records.

2. Advertising choices

When available, open the app's ⋮ menu → Ad privacy choices to review or withdraw your consent. Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID. For Google's retained data, see Google's privacy policy and controls.

3. Request deletion

Write to kwizustudio@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. For technical analytics, include your phone's make and model, interface language and approximate period of use. The app does not ask for your name or email, so those details alone do not identify its technical records. For purchase records, include the relevant transaction reference if available, without sending payment card information.

We will use the information to locate the relevant records and handle the request, including with our service providers where applicable. We aim to reply within 30 days. Google Play and RevenueCat may need to retain purchase records for their service or legal requirements. Deleting an entitlement record can affect automatic restoration; a deletion request is not a refund request.

4. Retention

Our PostHog technical measurements are retained for up to twelve months. Other providers apply their own retention rules. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and the services listed in our privacy policy.
