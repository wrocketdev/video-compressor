Compress Video support

Free video compression for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Contact

Email kwizustudio@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Please do not send private videos.

Getting started

Choose a video, select the target size or quality, then compress it. Preview the result before saving or sharing. Final size can vary. Originals are kept unless you explicitly use the space-saving feature and confirm deletion.

Common questions

Does it work offline? Video compression runs on your device. Technical analytics and this support page require a network connection.

Where is my video? Use Save to add the compressed copy to Photos or the gallery, or Share to send it to another app.

Why is storage still occupied? Deleted originals may remain in Photos’ Recently Deleted album or Android’s trash until you remove them there.

Is payment required? No. Version 1.8.0 and later has no ads, subscription or in-app purchases.
